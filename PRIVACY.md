# Privacy Policy for xTerminal Pro

**Last Updated: September 2026**

xTerminal Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices regarding your use of the xTerminal Pro desktop and mobile applications.

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
xTerminal Pro is a client-side terminal workstation. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, telemetry, analytics, or behavioral tracking data to third-party servers.

## 2. Credentials and Private Keys
All credentials, including:
- SSH passwords and private keys
- Telnet credentials
- Server connection bookmarks and profiles
- Terminal command history

are stored strictly **locally on your device** in encrypted storage. They never leave your device unless explicitly shared by you in a peer-to-peer multiplayer collaboration session.

## 3. Network Connections
xTerminal Pro only connects to:
- Remote servers and network hosts that you explicitly instruct the application to connect to (via SSH, SFTP, Telnet, Serial, or ADB).
- Localhost network bridge (127.0.0.1) for internal terminal emulation and process management.

## 4. Third-Party Services
If you choose to use the AI Assistant feature with your own Google Gemini API key, prompt data is sent directly to Google Gemini APIs according to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not intermediate or store these requests.
