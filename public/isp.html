Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting connected with Lyarinet.

What is the difference between Fiber (FTTH) and standard copper broadband?
Lyarinet uses Fiber to the Home (FTTH), meaning pure optical glass cables run directly from our 10G central nodes directly into your residence. Unlike traditional copper or coaxial cables, fiber transmits data at the speed of light, is completely immune to electrical interference, does not degrade over distance, and provides equal (symmetrical) upload and download speeds.
How long does installation and activation take?
Once you submit your request and our coverage team verifies your building or home drop, our certified fiber technician team installs the optical line and activates your Wi-Fi 6 GigaHub ONT router typically within 24 to 48 hours.
Are there any Fair Usage Policy (FUP) limits or data caps?
No! Lyarinet offers truly 100% unlimited data. You can download and upload terabytes of data every single month without any speed throttling, data caps, or hidden restrictions.
Do I get a static Public IP for hosting, servers, or CCTV?
Yes! Static dedicated public IPv4 and IPv6 addresses are readily available. Ultra Fiber and Gigabit Pro packages include complimentary static IP configuration upon request for remote servers, NAS backups, and security DVRs.
How do I pay my monthly bill?
You can pay effortlessly via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Kuickpay, 1Link Online Banking, Credit/Debit cards, or at our customer service centers. Instant automatic receipt and SMS confirmation are dispatched immediately.
