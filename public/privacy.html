Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026  |  Developer: Lyarinet

🛡️ Privacy Summary & Guarantee

xTerminal does not collect, log, sell, or transmit any of your personal data, server credentials, or terminal traffic. All SSH keys, passwords, and sessions remain strictly on your local device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the xTerminal software application across all supported platforms (Android, Linux/Snap, Windows, macOS). By using xTerminal, you agree to the transparent data practices described herein.

2. Data We Handle and Where It Stays

  • Server Credentials & Passwords: All hostnames, IP addresses, SSH private keys, passphrases, and configurations are stored securely and encrypted locally on your device. We have zero access to your infrastructure.
  • Terminal Buffers & Commands: Shell input and output stream directly between your device and your designated remote server via encrypted SSH or user-configured connections. No terminal logs are transmitted to any third-party or developer servers.
  • Multiplayer Live Sharing (Optional): When you explicitly create a real-time multiplayer sharing link, terminal buffers are routed ephemeral and real-time between authorized participants. No session history is retained once the session ends.

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

xTerminal only requests standard permissions strictly necessary for its core network terminal functionality:

Permission Required Purpose
INTERNET Connects to your remote SSH, Telnet, and SFTP servers.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Detects network transitions (WiFi/Cellular) for auto keep-alive reconnection.
STORAGE Imports/exports connection bookmarks and saves downloaded SFTP files.
USB / SERIAL Direct console connection to hardware switches, routers, and ADB devices.

4. Analytics & Advertising

xTerminal contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or user-profiling analytics libraries. Your developer workflow remains 100% confidential.
